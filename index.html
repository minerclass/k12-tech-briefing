A leadership brief you can use

Budget & continuity

Know your exposure.

Identify which current services and refresh projects depend on E-Rate. Build scenarios from verified local figures, without treating a proposal as a funding cut that has already happened.

Procurement & accountability

Keep the process defensible.

Assign review of filing dates, procurement paths, supporting records, and payment readiness. Keep responsibility visible without assuming that a state purchasing vehicle satisfies every requirement.

Use a one-minute cabinet or board briefing

As of the October 21 source snapshot, the court case, FCC policy reviews, and applicant work are separate tracks. Our team should continue applicable filings, verify local budget exposure, and monitor official developments. Proposals should not be presented as final requirements. Any local financial estimate needs a stated year, funding measure, and source.

Use the linked primary sources and adapt the wording to verified local conditions. This is a briefing template, not evidence that your team has completed those actions.

A useful distinction for your writing

Affordable connectivity and thoughtful classroom technology use are compatible goals. A district can question weak instructional uses while sustaining the network that supports its work.

Editorial interpretation. Funding records alone cannot establish whether screen use improves learning.

Translate the acronyms
USF / Universal Service Fund
The funding framework that includes E-Rate and other universal service programs.
USAC
Universal Service Administrative Company, the program administrator under FCC oversight.
NPRM / FNPRM
A notice asking for input on possible rules. It is not itself a final rule.
WC docket
A proceeding number. Use it to distinguish the two FCC reviews.
EPC / FRN
E-Rate Productivity Center / Funding Request Number.
Category One / Category Two
Connectivity to buildings / eligible internal network infrastructure.
CIPA
Children's Internet Protection Act. Its interpretation is part of the E-Rate review.
Contribution factor
A quarterly percentage used to calculate covered providers' USF contributions. It is not a school's E-Rate discount.

Use the right denominator.

That describes filings in its review. It does not measure public opinion, establish policy consensus, or determine the FCC's decision.
